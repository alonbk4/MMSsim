Real climate normals for your site

Fetches fifteen years of daily weather for one point from the Open-Meteo archive (ERA5 reanalysis, free, no key) and averages it into the twelve monthly numbers MMSsim actually uses. Everything happens in this page — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the only thing that leaves your browser is a latitude and a longitude.

Data © Open-Meteo, CC-BY-4.0, derived from ECMWF ERA5.
